## Master and Runtime rule

1. Generate and QA the batch in Google AI Studio with Leda.
2. Preserve exact narration word-for-word.
3. Export/split to one final WAV master per Scene, 24 kHz mono PCM 16-bit.
4. Listen to every WAV from start to finish and verify the final word is complete.
5. Encode approved masters to MP3 96 kbps mono for Runtime.
6. Runtime must contain 17 MP3 files and no story WAV masters.
